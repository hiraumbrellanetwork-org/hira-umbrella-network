About Hira Umbrella Network

Hira Umbrella Network is an officially recognized civil society umbrella operating in Somaliland. We pool technical expertise, resources, and community leadership to create sustainable impact for women, children, IDPs, and persons with disabilities.

Our Strategic Pillars

Our collaborative programs focus on core pillars aligned with international development standards.

1. Women Empowerment & Leadership

Promoting socio-economic self-reliance, leadership representation, and legal rights advocacy for women across Somaliland.

2. Child Welfare & Protection

Safeguarding children's rights, supporting education access, and establishing community protection mechanisms.

3. IDP Protection & Relief

Providing direct advocacy, emergency support, and sustainable development initiatives for internally displaced families.

4. Disability Rights & Inclusion

Championing equal accessibility, institutional policy changes, and anti-discrimination initiatives for persons with disabilities.

5. Policy Advocacy & Legal Reform

Engaging national stakeholders and international partners to promote policy frameworks that protect vulnerable groups.

6. Capacity Building for CSOs

Strengthening the institutional capacity, reporting standards, and operational efficiency of member organizations.
