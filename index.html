Uniting & Empowering Women in Somaliland

Hira Umbrella Network brings together 19 women-led organizations across Somaliland to advocate for women empowerment, child welfare, IDP protection, disability rights, and social inclusion.

Learn More

Our Key Focus Areas

Women & Children Empowerment

Strengthening women's leadership, socio-economic independence, and championing children's rights across communities.

Protection of IDPs & Vulnerable Groups

Providing advocacy, support, and sustainable development programs for internally displaced persons (IDPs) and marginalized communities.

Disability Rights & Social Inclusion

Promoting equal opportunities, accessibility, and anti-discrimination support for persons with disabilities in Somaliland.
